💥1 Overview
The Fuzzy Flexible Job Shop Scheduling Problem (FFJSP) inherits the characteristics of the Flexible Job Shop Scheduling Problem (FJSP), while adding flexibility in processing time and due dates. This paper imposes a constraint on the FJSP by introducing new job insertions and points out that the completion time of operations has a significant impact on re-scheduling solutions.
